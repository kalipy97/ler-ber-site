Who we are

A youth-led organization turning environmental awareness into practical local action.

We work with households, schools, markets, institutions and local stakeholders across Homa Bay town and surrounding communities. We see waste as a resource and an opportunity to build skills, green livelihoods and local enterprise.

Our approach: community participation, practical solutions, responsible resource use and lasting behaviour change. We welcome partnerships with government, civil society, schools, businesses, development partners and communities.

Vision

A clean, healthy and sustainable Homa Bay where communities manage resources responsibly and waste is valued.

Mission

Mobilize communities and partners to adopt integrated waste management, advance circular solutions and unlock youth-led green livelihoods.
