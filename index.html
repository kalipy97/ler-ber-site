Who we are

A youth-led organization turning environmental awareness into practical local action.

We work with households, schools, markets, institutions and local stakeholders across Homa Bay town and surrounding communities. We see waste as a resource and an opportunity to build skills, green livelihoods and local enterprise.

Our approach: community participation, practical solutions, responsible resource use and lasting behaviour change. We welcome partnerships with government, civil society, schools, businesses, development partners and communities.

Vision

A clean, healthy and sustainable Homa Bay where communities manage resources responsibly and waste is valued.

Mission

Mobilize communities and partners to adopt integrated waste management, advance circular solutions and unlock youth-led green livelihoods.

What we are building

From community awareness to resource recovery and green jobs. These are the programmes we are working to deliver in full. The volunteer programme below is our first step.

Community waste management

Support collection, segregation and responsible disposal in neighbourhoods and institutions.

School environmental action

Promote colour-coded bins and environmental clubs.

Lakeside and market clean-ups

Recover plastic litter and engage fish traders, fisherfolk and nearby communities.

Advocacy and sensitization

Reach people through barazas, churches, local radio and stakeholder events.

Our waste-to-value pathway

Community organic waste becomes useful outputs.

  1. Segregate

    Separate food and market organics at the source.

  2. Bioconvert

    Feed organics to black soldier fly larvae.

  3. Recover

    Larvae can support animal feed. Frass can improve soils.

To scale up, we are prioritizing mechanized substrate preparation, better infrastructure, product quality, packaging, certification guidance and market links.

Our volunteer programme: 90 days for a cleaner Homa Bay

A voluntary lakeside clean-up, plastic recovery, advocacy and awareness programme to protect Lake Victoria. It is our starting point while we mobilize resources and partners for the actual work. It is not a one-off event: visible clean-up action comes with sustained behaviour change and partnership-building.

Where

Homa Bay Fish Market, Oginga Beach and surrounding lakeside communities.

Who we engage

Fish traders, fisherfolk, beach and market leaders, residents, volunteers and local stakeholders.

Our goal

A cleaner lakeside and stronger community ownership of waste reduction, reuse, segregation, recycling and responsible disposal.

  1. Mobilize and launchDays 1–30

    Orient volunteers, identify litter hotspots, engage beach and market leaders, launch clean-ups and public awareness.

  2. Engage and intensifyDays 31–60

    Continue clean-ups, sensitize fish traders, fisherfolk and residents, promote segregation, connect plastics to recycling partners.

  3. ConsolidateDays 61–90

    Sustain activities, document results, recognize partners and volunteers, share lessons and a post-programme action plan.

How we will measure progress

We will document participation, waste recovery and community engagement throughout.

  • Volunteer participation
  • Number of clean-up sessions
  • Waste collected by type and weight
  • People reached through awareness
  • Partners engaged
  • Before-and-after photos
  • Final 90-day report

The programme supports Sustainable Development Goals 3, 4, 8, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 17.
