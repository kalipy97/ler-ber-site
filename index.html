Clean shores. A living lake. Waste turned to worth.

Ler Ber is a youth-led community organization in Homa Bay County, Kenya. We are starting with a volunteer clean-up programme on the lakeside while we mobilize the resources for our full waste-to-value work, all to protect Lake Victoria.
