What we are building

From community awareness to resource recovery and green jobs. These are the programmes we are working to deliver in full. Our volunteer programme is the first step.

1

Lake Victoria clean-up

Oginga Beach and lakeside communities

Shoreline clean-ups and plastic recovery, with awareness so the lakeside stays clean after volunteers leave.

2

Fish market clean-up

Homa Bay Fish Market

Working with fish traders and market leaders to clean the market and build habits that keep waste out of the lake.

3

Estate clean-ups

Makongeni, Sofia, Shauri Yako and other estates

Neighbourhood clean-up days that start waste collection and separation at household level.

4

Black soldier fly project

Community organic waste to useful products

Turning food and market organics into animal feed and a soil booster, creating skills and green jobs. The clean-ups and waste separation above feed this work.

  1. Segregate

    Separate food and market organics at the source.

  2. Bioconvert

    Feed organics to black soldier fly larvae.

  3. Recover

    Larvae can support animal feed. Frass can improve soils.

To scale up, we are prioritizing mechanized substrate preparation, better infrastructure, product quality, packaging, certification guidance and market links.
